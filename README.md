# Form Filler

A small web app that fills Great Eastern Life Malaysia forms from an ePartner "Policy Detail Information" printout.

**Forms available**
- Appointment / Change of Nominee(s) / Trustee(s) — NBZ-FNOMI-V07-082025
- Obstetrics & Gynaecology Report (header only) — NBZ-QOBGY-V03-082025a

## How to use
1. Save the client's ePartner *Policy Detail Information* page as PDF.
2. Open the app, choose the printout and the form.
3. Check the details, fill in what the portal does not have (you can paste nominee details from a message).
4. Create the filled form, check every page in the preview, then save the PDF.

## Privacy
The printout is read inside your own browser. Client details are **not uploaded** anywhere and are not stored in this repository. Answers you type are kept only in your browser on that device ("Forget them" removes them).

## Notes
- Independent tool for agency use. Not made, endorsed or supported by Great Eastern.
- Always check the filled form before submission. Signatures, dates and medical answers are completed by hand.
- The app never guesses: anything not on the printout is left blank and listed.
